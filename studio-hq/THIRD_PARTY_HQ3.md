# Third-party audio resources used by Studio HQ 0.3

The repository does not redistribute the large sample libraries below. `upgrade_hq3.sh` downloads them from their upstream distribution locations into `/var/lib/musiclite/studio-hq`.

## DrumGizmo kits

- CrocellKit 1.1 — https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:crocellkit
- DRSKit 2.1 — https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:drskit

These kits are published under the Creative Commons terms described by the upstream project. The kit pages require attribution in contexts where other performance credits are presented. Keep the generated `manifest.json` with production records and review upstream terms before commercial distribution.

## Unreal Instruments / SFZ Instruments catalogue

- Metal GTX — https://unreal-instruments.wixsite.com/unreal-instruments/metal-gtx
- Standard Guitar — https://unreal-instruments.wixsite.com/unreal-instruments/standard-guitar
- Standard Bass — https://unreal-instruments.wixsite.com/unreal-instruments/standard-bass

Downloads remain subject to the upstream instrument/license terms. They are not committed to this repository.

## Voxengo impulse responses

- Free Reverb Impulse Responses — https://www.voxengo.com/impulses/

The installer downloads the upstream impulse-response archive directly. Do not redistribute those files from this repository; follow Voxengo's current terms for usage and redistribution.
